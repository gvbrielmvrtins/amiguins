Paula's photo image 3 is likeness reference only: long curly golden light-brown hair, side part, dark brows, friendly broad smile, small round earrings, peace necklace. Image 4 defines clothing and action only: green white striped open shirt, cream top, beige shorts, bare feet, using both hands on wooden shovel with metal blade to dig sand, slight forward lean. Avoid realistic face, detailed realistic teeth, realistic nose/lips or long fashion-model legs. Match Nathan's eye proportions and Kauana's body proportions, keep adult woman recognizable. Complete isolated figure and shovel, upright three-quarter game view. Transparent background, no scenery, no text, no glow.
