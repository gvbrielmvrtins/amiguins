## Prompt executado

Create ONE integrated game asset: a large spacious circular town-center public square, elevated isometric view with ground axes +/-30 degrees and vertical objects upright. References 1 cinema and 2 tree are ONLY explicit visual style references: match their polished retro-pop cartoon dark outlines, layered construction, controlled highlights, warm cream/coral/yellow and blue palette, richly drawn green foliage. Entire circular plaza seen as broad ellipse in perspective, no rectangular diorama platform, no raised slab. Low flush cream stone paving with subtle cobblestone texture and circular decorative bands. Generous open central walking space, elegant blue water tiered fountain at center occupying only 15% area. Around perimeter six wooden benches, four blue decorative streetlamps, spaced leafy trees, lush flowerbeds, a small open gazebo near rear edge, discreet bins. Four broad entrances and open circulation between elements. It must feel like a major city-center square, not a crowded tiny garden. All these objects and paving comprise one cohesive single image. No people, no buildings beyond gazebo, no text, no surroundings. Full asset uncropped with transparent background beyond circular boundary, minimal margins.
