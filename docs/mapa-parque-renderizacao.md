Image2 tree reference for warm natural materials and detailed shading only. Image3 bench reference for matching honey-brown wood and dark green hardware. Subject: short straight wooden picket fence panel, two sturdy end posts, six evenly spaced upright wooden slats with gently rounded tops, two horizontal support rails, subtle woodgrain, dark green small bolts. Low welcoming public park fence. Elevated isometric view, long ground axis descends to RIGHT at exactly +30 degrees; posts perfectly vertical. Both post feet aligned along the same +30-degree baseline. Entire isolated panel, ample transparent margins. No lawn, ground tile, trees, buildings, people, signs or text. Upper-left lighting, controlled highlights. This segment will be repeated around an existing park.

Parque junto às construções novas, mantendo a implantação aprovada, árvores plantadas diretamente no chão e circulação livre, sem reintroduzir caminhos. Elementos gerados individualmente com fundo transparente e integrados em profundidade no mapa.
