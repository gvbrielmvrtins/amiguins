## Prompt do entregador

Use case: illustration-story. Transparent isolated full-body pizza delivery man carrying a comically tall tower of eight closed cardboard pizza boxes stacked in both arms, tower reaching above his head, face visible to left of boxes. Red cap and red polo, blue trousers, sneakers, warm friendly expressive face, walking carefully toward screen right. Use Nathan and Gabriel reference images strictly as style references: bold clean black contours, expressive cartoon eyes, slightly large head, controlled cel shading, same polished cartoon identity, but a new unique face. Boxes detailed folded cardboard cream and red rims, no text. Elevated three-quarter map view with feet on isometric ground axes ±30 degrees, consistent light upper left. Only one man and his boxes, entire silhouette, true transparent background, no floor, no scene, no other props.

## Prompt da fatia

Use case: stylized-concept. Single loose triangular slice of pepperoni pizza as transparent isolated map sprite. Match the attached tavern, cinema and tree illustration style: carefully defined dark outlines, rich golden browned crust, molten yellow cheese, red pepperoni circles, controlled warm highlights and cel shadows. Elevated isometric three-quarter view, slice lying flat on the LEFT sloping terracotta roof face of the referenced tavern, point toward lower left and curved thick crust toward upper right. Thus visible cheese surface should have the same plane orientation as that roof slope; no actual roof in output. Entire one slice, appetizing recognizable shape, subtle tight contact shadow, no plate, no boxes, no floor, no people, no text, genuine transparent background.
