## Prompt da edição

Edit this cartoon isometric noticeboard. Preserve EXACT frame, coral/cream carved wood surround, blue feet, black outlines, perspective, framing and transparent background. Replace the four little pinned scraps with ONE large warm ivory paper poster pinned neatly to the cork inside the existing frame, leaving generous visible cork margin on all sides. Poster edges must follow the same planar perspective as the cork: vertical side edges, top/bottom slope down to the right. Add two small blue pushpins near upper corners, subtle contact shadow, slight curled lower corner and clean blank paper center. The poster has NO text or decoration, blank surface for subsequent typography. Do NOT cover or change the surrounding frame or legs. Crisp existing polished cartoon style, no halo or background.
