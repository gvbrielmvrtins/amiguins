# Isolamento das ruínas

Oito trechos independentes editáveis em `components/ruins-caution-tape.tsx` acompanham os quatro lados do terreno. Os trechos de fundo ficam atrás das ruínas; os frontais ficam à frente. A projeção ajusta o eixo de chão mantendo as estacas verticais. Duas orientações ilustradas preservam a leitura do texto sem espelhamento.

Assets gerados via imagegen integrado, transparência preservada:

- `public/images/modular/faixa-nao-ultrapasse-v01.png`
- `public/images/modular/faixa-nao-ultrapasse-oposta-v01.png`

Referências explícitas de estilo: cinema e árvore originais.

## Prompts executados

Thin flexible yellow tape, naturally slightly sagging and twisting, black diagonal warning stripes at ends and clear bold black Portuguese wording repeated along the tape: 'NÃO ULTRAPASSE'. Tape must look like physical flexible plastic ribbon, NOT a solid barricade board. Stakes have pointed ends planted at same ground level, no large feet. Elevated isometric view: ground line from LOWER LEFT to UPPER RIGHT, minus30 degrees; stakes vertical. Entire object uncropped. Cinema and tree explicit STYLE references for polished retro-pop cartoon map, defined dark outlines, natural wood grain and controlled shadows. Actual transparent background. No ground platform, roads, people, ruins, additional fencing or watermark. One straight reusable section only.

Create the matching OPPOSITE ISOMETRIC ORIENTATION of this exact caution tape asset. Same yellow flexible ribbon with black diagonal stripes and two repeated readable 'NÃO ULTRAPASSE' labels, same three short wooden stakes and same retro-pop illustration style. Now the long ground axis runs from UPPER LEFT to LOWER RIGHT (+30 degrees) instead of rising right. Stakes remain vertical. Crucially all Portuguese text remains correctly readable, never mirrored. Naturally slightly sagging tape. Whole object, actual transparent background, no extra objects, no floor. Preserve materials, colors, proportions and finish.
