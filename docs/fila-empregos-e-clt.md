# Fila de empregos e carteira de trabalho

Dois sprites móveis independentes via imagegen integrado, fundos transparentes. Balcões do mercado observados antes da geração: voltados para baixo/direita; fila estende-se para baixo/direita com pessoas olhando para cima/esquerda. Referências de personagens Nathan e Gabriel.

Arquivos: `public/images/modular/fila-empregos-v01.png` e `public/images/modular/carteira-trabalho-avulsa-v01.png`.

## Prompt da fila

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE sprite of five diverse ADULT job seekers waiting in a single file queue outside a Brazilian job-market storefront. Image1 is storefront ORIENTATION reference only do NOT draw building. Its service counters face bottom right. Queue line ground axis must extend from UPPER LEFT (front of queue) to LOWER RIGHT (back of queue), downward diagonal approximately +30 degrees screen angle. All people turned to face UPPER LEFT toward service counter, profiles or three-quarter backs visible, NOT front facing camera, not sideways lineup. Distinct adult men and women, mixed skin tones, everyday casual and smart casual clothing, one older adult, holding CV paper folders and blue Brazilian work booklets. Feet correctly staggered on same diagonal line and characters do not obscure each other excessively. Image2 Nathan and image3 Gabriel are explicit cartoon style references: black defined outlines enlarged heads expressive eyes controlled cel shading. Do not reuse their faces. No text necessary. No floor, no building, transparent background. Whole queue including feet.

## Prompt da carteira

Use case: stylized-concept. One isolated Brazilian traditional blue Carteira de Trabalho booklet, colloquially CLT, as movable prop in cartoon isometric city game. Closed navy blue slightly worn leatherlike cover, small gold Brazilian coat-of-arms style emblem centered, gold lettering "CARTEIRA DE TRABALHO" on two lines and small "BRASIL" below. Cream page edges visible. Lying at elevated isometric angle, floor axes ±30deg, full object, no hands no other objects no floor. Explicit original cinema reference for bold dark worked outlines clean cel shadows and highlights retro pop finish. Genuine transparent background, readable recognizable blue work booklet, no photorealism.
